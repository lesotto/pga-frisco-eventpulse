Sponsor ledger · ROI summary

One score. One ROI answer.

The Event Outcome Score (EOS) rolls the deck's four ledger measures (exposure, behavior, sentiment, redemptions) into one 0–100 number per sponsor, scored per dollar invested. ROI is the value the ledger can attribute, against what the sponsor spent to activate. The method is fixed now; the numbers arrive with championship-week data.

How the score and ROI work

Method · fixed
Event Outcome Score (0–100)

Each ledger measure is taken per dollar invested and scaled from 30 (weakest in the field) to 100 (strongest). Sentiment uses its net score. Then weighted:

  • Exposure · impressions per $30%
  • Behavior · visits, dwell, taps per $25%
  • Sentiment · net score15%
  • Redemptions · offers claimed per $30%
ROI

(Attributed value − Investment) ÷ Investment

Attributed value = media value (impressions × $28 CPM) + engagement value (behaviors × $1.10) + redemption margin (redemptions × $42 order × 60%).

Bands

80+ Strong · 65–79 Solid · 50–64 Watch · <50 Under

Rates are EventPulse working assumptions, to be agreed with each sponsor before championship week. The score and ROI move together because both are scored per dollar; the real correlation will be reported from championship-week data.
